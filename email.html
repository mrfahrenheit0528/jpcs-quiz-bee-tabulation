Subject: Overview of the JPCS-CSPC Automated Quiz Bee Tabulation System

Dear JFINEX CSPC Chapter,

We are pleased to inform you that the Automated Quiz Bee Tabulation System, developed by the Junior Philippine Computer Society - CSPC Chapter, is now fully operational and ready for your upcoming event.

This system has been custom-architected to meet the specific, rigorous demands of high-level academic competitions. Below is a technical overview of the system's capabilities and the logic governing the competition flow.

The "Hybrid" Scoring Engine

This system features a specialized Hybrid Logic designed specifically for your tournament rules:

1. Elimination Phase (Cumulative Scoring)
Scores from preliminary rounds (Easy, Average) are cumulative. The system automatically tracks the running total to determine the top qualifiers who will advance to the finals.

2. Final Phase (Clean Slate Protocol)
Upon reaching the Final Round (Difficult), all previous scores are reset to zero. The winners are determined solely by their performance in the Final Round, ensuring a "best of the best" showdown.

⚔️ Advanced Tie-Breaking Mechanics

The system eliminates the need for manual computation or debate during ties. It employs an Iterative & Recursive Tie-Breaking Algorithm:

  • Automatic Detection (Cumulative Rounds): The system instantly detects ties at critical cut-off points (e.g., 3 schools fighting for the last qualifying slot).
  • Recursive "Clincher" Rounds (Final Round): If contestants tie again during a tie-breaker (e.g., Clincher 1), the system automatically generates a new round (Clincher 2) exclusively for the remaining tied contestants.
  • Strict Ranking: In the Final Round, the system enforces a strict ranking order (no shared ranks). It will force tie-breakers until a distinct 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winner is identified.

Mission Control & Transparency

To ensure the integrity of the event, the system is divided into three secure interfaces:

  • Admin Mission Control: A real-time dashboard that allows us to monitor answering progress, lock/unlock rounds, and trigger "Sudden Death" questions instantly (Based on Guidlines).
  • Tabulator Panel: A restricted data-entry interface for tabulators, ensuring they can only input scores for the active round.
  • Live Leaderboard: A dynamic audience display that updates in real-time, featuring automated phase detection (hiding cumulative history during the finals to build suspense).

Official Reporting

Immediately after the event, the system generates secured PDF Official Results. These documents include dynamic signature blocks for all tabulators and the head administrator, ready for printing and archiving.
